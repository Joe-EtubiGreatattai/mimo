3. What Mimo does

Mimo checks how much free space your computer has. When space runs low, or when you ask him, he looks for files he considers safe to remove in the places you have switched on, such as app caches, old downloads of installers, old WhatsApp media and developer caches. Depending on the place and your settings, he either deletes those files permanently or moves them to the Bin (Mac) or Recycle Bin (Windows).

Mimo decides what is "junk" from rules about where files are and how old they are. He can't know what each file means to you. A file that looks like a cache or an old download may still matter to you, and apps may need to download or rebuild what he removes, which can use time and data.

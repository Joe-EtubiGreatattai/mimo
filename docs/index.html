Mimo Download

Mimo keeps your computer from running out of space.

Mimo sits in your Mac's menu bar or your Windows tray and checks your free space every minute. When it gets low, he gathers the caches and old WhatsApp media he can clear, asks for your OK, then tells you how much room he got back.

Watching your storage

When Mimo steps in

Every 60 seconds Mimo reads how much space macOS says you can actually use. Nothing happens while you're in the green. By default he speaks up at 25 GB and steps in at 15 GB. You can move that line anywhere from 5 GB to 500 GB in his settings.

Install

  1. Download and drag

    Open the disk image and drag Mimo into your Applications folder.

  2. Open him once

    Mimo isn't from the App Store, so the first time, right-click him and choose Open, then confirm.

  3. Allow notifications

    Click Allow when macOS asks, so he can check in with you.

  4. Give Full Disk Access

    In System Settings › Privacy & Security › Full Disk Access, switch Mimo on so he can reach WhatsApp's folder.

On Windows, run Mimo-Setup.exe. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. Mimo appears in the system tray next to the clock.

Questions

Can Mimo ask me before he deletes anything?

Yes, and that's how he starts. When space runs low he puts everything he wants to remove on one Review screen, grouped by type with sizes and the biggest files listed. Untick anything you want to keep and click Clean selected. If you'd rather not be asked, switch him to Clean automatically in Settings.

Is there a Windows version?

Yes. Download the Windows installer and run it. Mimo lives in your system tray, next to the clock. Because Mimo is new and not yet signed with a Microsoft certificate, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway. On Windows he looks after temporary files, browser caches, WhatsApp Desktop media, Teams, Slack and Discord caches, crash dumps and developer caches.

Can I control Mimo from my phone?

Yes. In Mimo, open Settings and click Pair a phone, then scan the QR code. Your phone gets the Mimo Remote app, which you can add to your Home Screen. It shows your Mac's free space, lets you approve what Mimo found, change when he steps in and switch places on or off. Your phone sees totals and categories only. File names never leave your Mac.

Will Mimo delete my photos or documents?

No. He only works in the places listed above, and only the ones you've switched on. WhatsApp media is moved to your Bin, and only when it's more than 30 days old, so you can restore anything he moved.

Does he send my data anywhere?

Your files never leave your Mac. Mimo sends two kinds of anonymous counts: one ping the first time he runs (a random ID he makes up, his version, your system version and chip type), and after each clean, how much space he cleared in each category, like "App caches: 2.1 GB". Never file names, folder names or anything you made. You can switch both off in Settings › Privacy. His activity log stays in your Library › Logs folder.

Does emptying the Bin matter?

Yes. WhatsApp media in the Bin still takes up space until you empty it. Caches are removed straight away.

Will he slow my Mac down?

Checking free space takes a fraction of a second, once a minute. Cleaning runs at low priority in the background.

How do I stop him?

Open his window, click the menu next to Check, and choose Quit Mimo. To remove him, drag Mimo from Applications to the Bin.

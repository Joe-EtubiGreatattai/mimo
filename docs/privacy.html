Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

In short: Mimo cleans your computer without uploading your files. He never sends file names, folder names or file contents anywhere. He shares a few anonymous numbers with our server, such as which version he runs and how much space he has cleared, and you can switch that off in Settings › Privacy. If you pair a phone, a storage summary goes through our server to that phone. We don't sell data, show ads or use tracking cookies.

1. Who we are

Mimo is made by Great Attai, an independent developer based in Abuja, Nigeria, who is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: greatattai442442@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Mimo apps for Mac and Windows, Mimo Remote, the website and the Mimo server at mimo.techtree.lifestyle. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and, for people in the European Economic Area and the UK, the main principles of the GDPR.

2. What stays on your computer

Everything Mimo does with your files happens on your computer. His map of your folders, the list of files he finds, his activity log and your settings are stored only on your computer. Mimo never uploads your files, file names, folder names, file contents, photos or messages. He reads WhatsApp's media folder only to check file dates and sizes, never to look inside your chats.

3. Anonymous counts the apps send

Unless you switch off Share anonymous counts in Settings › Privacy, Mimo sends the following to our server. It is tied to a random install ID made on your computer, not to your name, email or account.

WhenWhat is sent
First launch and after each updateRandom install ID, app version, operating system version, chip type (for example Apple silicon or Intel) and whether it's Mac or Windows.
After each cleanHow much space was cleared in each category (for example "App caches: 2.1 GB"), how the clean started (automatic, by you, after Review, or from a phone), the app version and the time. If you cleaned your own folders, they are reported only as "Your folders", without their names.
Once a dayYour "clean below" level, whether "ask me first" is on, and your disk's free and total space in GB.

We use these numbers to count installs, see which versions people run, and show totals such as "space Mimo has claimed back" on the website and our stats page.

4. Mimo Remote (if you pair a phone)

Mimo Remote is optional and off until you pair a phone. When it's on, your computer sends the following to our server so your phone can show it:

  • your computer's name as set in macOS or Windows (this may include your first name, for example "Ada's MacBook"; you can change it in your system settings);
  • free and total space, the categories Mimo can clean and their sizes, the places you've switched on (your own folders appear under their folder name), Mimo's settings and his recent activity messages;
  • a random device ID and a secret, which we store only as a scrambled hash.

For the phone we store a scrambled hash of its access token, a simple label ("iPhone", "Android phone" or "Browser") and when it was paired. Pairing codes are stored as hashes and expire after 10 minutes. Commands you send from the phone, like "clean now", are deleted automatically after 7 days. Unpairing all phones in Mimo's Settings removes the phones' access.

5. Update checks

From version 1.5.0 Mimo checks GitHub for new versions every few hours and downloads updates from GitHub. GitHub sees your IP address when this happens, as with any download. You can switch automatic updates off in Settings › Updates.

6. The website

  • No tracking cookies, no ads, no analytics services.
  • When you click a download button, the site records which button was clicked and when, with no ID attached.
  • If you tap "I want Mimo for Windows", the site saves a random ID in your browser's local storage so you're only counted once, and sends that random ID to our server.
  • The site shows live totals from our server, such as space cleared.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses fonts from Google Fonts. Both receive your IP address and browser details when you visit, under their own privacy policies.

7. Why we use data

We use the data above to run Mimo and Mimo Remote, deliver updates, keep the server secure and working, understand how Mimo is used so we can improve him, and show public totals that contain no personal data. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in running and improving a free app in a way that respects your privacy and, for Mimo Remote, providing the feature you asked for. We don't sell or rent data, use it for advertising, or make automated decisions about you.

8. Who else handles data

We share data only with services that help us run Mimo:

  • MongoDB Atlas (MongoDB, Inc.) stores the server's database.
  • Our server host runs the Mimo server. Like most web servers it keeps short-lived access logs, which include IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc., part of Microsoft) hosts the website, downloads and updates.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC) serves the website's fonts.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or as part of a transfer if Mimo gets a new owner, who would have to respect this policy.

9. Where data is stored

These providers may store data outside Nigeria, for example in the United States or Europe. Where the law requires, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers, such as the providers' standard contractual terms.

10. How long we keep it

  • Anonymous install, cleanup and daily check-in records are kept while Mimo is running as a service, so totals stay correct. We may delete or combine older records at any time.
  • Mimo Remote data is kept while the computer is paired, and updated as it changes. Pairing codes expire after 10 minutes. Commands are deleted after 7 days.
  • Server access logs are rotated and deleted automatically after a short period.
  • If Mimo is shut down, we'll delete the server's data.

11. Security

All traffic to our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Secrets and tokens are stored only as hashes, the server limits how often it can be called, and file information never leaves your computer. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect the little data we hold. If a breach affects your personal data, we'll notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

12. Your choices and rights

  • Switch off Share anonymous counts in Mimo › Settings › Privacy to stop sending counts.
  • Unpair all phones in Mimo › Settings to stop Mimo Remote.
  • Switch off automatic updates in Mimo › Settings › Updates.
  • Uninstalling Mimo stops everything he sends.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict how we use it, withdraw consent, and ask for a copy. Because we don't know who you are, we may need your install ID to find your records. On Mac you can see it by running defaults read com.great.mimo installId in Terminal. On Windows it's in the settings.json file in Mimo's app data folder. Email greatattai442442@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

13. Children

Mimo is not aimed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you think a child has shared personal data with us, contact us and we'll delete it.

14. Changes

We'll update this policy if Mimo starts handling data differently, and change the "Last updated" date. For important changes we'll also mention them in the release notes or the app.
